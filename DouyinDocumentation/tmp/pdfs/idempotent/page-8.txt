To support this behavior we need to retain knowledge of the initial idempotent request in our 
service. However, it’s impractical to r etain that knowledge indefinitely . Indefinite retention 
could also have an undesirable impact on the customer experience if some future request 
identifier collides with one used much earlier. Requirements for retention vary across services 
and service resources. We have found that, for EC2 instances, it works to limit the time period 
to the lifetime of the resource, plus an interval after which it is reasonable to assume that any 
late arriving requests would either have arrived or would no longer be valid.
