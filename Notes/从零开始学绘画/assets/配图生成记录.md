# 配图生成记录

使用方式：内置 image_gen 工具生成；头像图经过一次针对平涂阶段的编辑。未使用 CLI/API 回退。

三张最终图片均保存在此目录，由上一级学习指南通过相对路径引用。它们是以洛琪希为主题的教学示意，不是官方角色设定稿或逐帧对齐的动画素材。

## 图片与生成提示词

### 01-portrait-process.png

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Create a high-resolution landscape raster illustration for a Chinese absolute-beginner digital drawing guide. A clean six-panel educational sheet, 3 columns by 2 rows, read left to right then next row. Subject: Roxy Migurdia from Mushoku Tensei, recognizable blue hair, two blue braids, blue eyes, large brown witch hat and modest cream and brown mage outfit. Wholesome cute bust portrait, same front-facing head placement and proportions in every panel. Show actual additive steps, not six unrelated portraits. 01: only blue pencil circle cranial mass, simplified jaw, curved vertical centerline and eye-level guideline, no details. 02: same construction with simple eyes nose mouth and ears accurately placed. 03: same face with hair blocked into large locks and braids, a simple hat cone and elliptical brim. 04: clean approachable black lineart of completed same bust, no shading, construction erased. 05: same lineart with flat blue, brown, cream skin colors only. 06: same flat colors plus restrained hard-edged cel shadows consistent with light from upper left, gentle smile. Appearance and orientation remain consistent. Warm white paper background, generous negative space, thin panel dividers, blue construction lines, readable linework. Small number labels only '01', '02', '03', '04', '05', '06'. NO other text, no logo, no watermark, no software UI. Make intermediate drawings visibly simple and achievable, no ornate decoration or painting textures. Hat and braids fit comfortably in each finished panel. Diagram illustrates process, not an official model sheet.
```

### 02-expressions-blink.png

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Generate a high-resolution wide teaching illustration for beginners learning anime facial expressions. Clean white background, six equal panels arranged 3 columns and 2 rows, labels only 01 through 06. Same Roxy Migurdia from Mushoku Tensei in every panel: recognizable blue bangs, two blue braids, blue eyes, modest cream mage collar. No hat so eyebrows and forehead visible. Consistent frontal bust portrait, identical scale, head position and hair silhouette; do not tilt or turn the head. Simple polished cel-colored anime illustration, clear facial linework suitable to copy. Top row: 01 calm neutral, eyebrows relaxed, eyes normally open, small relaxed mouth; 02 gentle happy smile with softened eyelids, slight lifted cheeks and gentle curved mouth; 03 mild surprise with raised brows, wider eyes and small open round mouth. Bottom row: 04 neutral fully open eyes; 05 neutral HALF-CLOSED upper eyelids visibly halfway over irises; 06 neutral fully closed eyes in gentle curved lines. For bottom row only eyelids change; mouth, head, shoulders, hair remain identical. The lower row is blink closure phases (opening uses reverse order), not different emotions. Avoid exaggerated crying, hearts, sparkles, intense blush, dramatic effects, props. Educational comparison and wholesome cute character appeal. Only numerals 01 02 03 04 05 06, no other text or watermark.
```

### 03-pose-construction.png

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Create a high-resolution landscape beginner anime drawing tutorial sheet, 3 columns x 2 rows with generous white space. Each column teaches one simple full-body pose of Roxy Migurdia from Mushoku Tensei, with blue braided hair, brown witch hat, fully covering cream and brown mage robe, skirt and boots. Wholesome friendly clothed character. Three columns: 01 standing and waving hello with one hand beside face, weight over a planted leg; 02 standing holding a closed book gently against chest with both arms, slightly inward shoulders; 03 inquisitive gentle head tilt, arms relaxed in front, stable standing feet. Top row: super simplified clothed figure construction of that exact pose using head sphere, ribcage oval, pelvis box and single lines for arms and legs, small simple hand shapes, one blue action line; no detailed anatomy or shading. Bottom row: finished simple cel-colored anime character with exactly matching pose and proportion, flat colors plus modest shadows, clear readable silhouette, cute restrained facial expression. Full hat and both feet visible in every finished figure. Top and bottom pairs must align and clearly depict same pose. Body proportions consistent across columns. Correct limbs and hand anatomy, hands with five fingers where visible; no extra limbs or floating feet. Label each column only '01', '02', '03'. No prose, no captions, no software interface, no logos, no watermark. Useful as a study of gesture and large forms rather than polished poster art.
```

## 头像图的最终编辑提示词

```text
Edit this six-panel beginner drawing tutorial. Change ONLY panel 05 (bottom middle). Make panel 05 a genuine flat-color stage: remove ALL lighting shadows, skin gradients, hair highlights, reflected light and shaded braid facets. Keep the exact existing lineart and shape boundaries. Fill each material region with one uniform base color: flat blue hair, flat light skin, flat brown hat, flat cream and brown clothing. Black outlines and pupil/iris local colors may remain. Panels 01,02,03,04,06 and all panel lines/numbers remain pixel-composition consistent and unchanged. Panel 06 must remain shaded so the pedagogical contrast between flat color and shading is clear. No other changes.
```
